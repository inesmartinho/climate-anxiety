# Climate anxiety in data

Interactive web experience for the "Make sense" workshop (Master Digital Experience Design, ECAL).

- `index.html`: the 3D globe "map of emotions" (single self-contained file, data embedded), the main page
- `scroll.html`: the scrolling data experience (single self-contained file, data embedded)
- `data/`: the source CSVs

## Data sources
Yale Program on Climate Change Communication (psychological distress tables, 2023; Climate Change in the American Mind, Fall 2025; Global Warming's Six Americas, 2010), Hickman et al., The Lancet Planetary Health (2021), Oxford English Dictionary, UNDP climate dictionary, Éducation 21 / revue durable (Vaud youth survey), WHO Europe, Cambridge Global Mental Health review. Full links are listed at the bottom of the page.

## Limits
Self-reported feelings and screening scores, not diagnoses. Mostly US adults and a ten-country youth sample, not a world estimate. Vocabulary years are earliest-known documented uses, not coinage dates.

The globe map's country shapes and the gaps between grey land dots are my own rough drawings, not an official border dataset; small countries and islands are not separated.
