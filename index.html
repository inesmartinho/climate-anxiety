Solastalgia

An emotional geography of how we inhabit a changing world and the loss of a shared horizon.

solastalgia noun · the distress caused by environmental change while you are still at home: a feeling of loss for a place that is changing around you. Coined in 2003 by the philosopher Glenn Albrecht, from Latin solacium (comfort) and Greek -algia (pain).
